Reading Arbitrum...

Three steps. That is all.

  1. Freeze

    Pick BTC or ETH and an amount. Pay the fee. You get a freeze card in your wallet. It remembers today's price.

  2. Wait

    BTC freezes last 7 to 14 days. ETH freezes last 60 to 90 days. Nothing else is owed while you wait.

  3. Use it or let it go

    If the price is higher, the freeze pays you the difference in USDC. If it is not, you walk away and lose only the fee.

Look up a freeze card.

Try 16684 (an ETH freeze) or 16681 (a BTC freeze).

If the price ends below the frozen price, the card is worth nothing and the fee is gone. That fee is the most you can lose. Freeze does not predict prices or tell anyone to buy. Hegic's pool on Arbitrum sells each freeze and sets the fee, and the card goes straight to your own wallet.
