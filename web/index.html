How you use it

Just tell your agent.

Freeze is a plugin for PayBox, the wallet your AI agent can use. No new app, no exchange account. You talk, PayBox asks you to approve, the card lands in your wallet.

  1. Connect PayBox

    Add PayBox to Claude or ChatGPT. In Claude: Settings, Connectors, add https://api.paybox.sh/mcp.

  2. Turn on Freeze

    In PayBox, open Plugins and enable Freeze. Keep a few dollars of ETH on Arbitrum; Freeze swaps a little into USDC for the fee when needed.

  3. Say what you want

    "BTC dipped and my salary lands in 7 days. Use Freeze to lock today's price on $20 of BTC." Approve in PayBox and you are done. If BTC rises, ask your agent to cash the card before it ends.

Why not just buy?

Because the money isn't here yet.

Freeze

A card pays only when it is cashed before its end date, and only if the price is above the frozen price. Otherwise the fee is gone, and that fee is the most you can lose. Freeze does not predict prices or tell anyone to buy.
