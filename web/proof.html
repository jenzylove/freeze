What is proven, and what is not yet.

The market is real

Every Freeze is a call option bought from Hegic's pool on Arbitrum. The fees on the home page are read live from that pool, not invented. Each option is an NFT in the buyer's wallet. Anyone can look one up on the card page, for example card 16684 (ETH) or card 16681 (BTC).

A real freeze on Arbitrum mainnet, through PayBox

Live on mainnet

On 17 October 2026 a PayBox user asked their agent in plain words to freeze $20 of BTC for 7 days. The agent called Freeze, and each transaction appeared in the PayBox window for the user to approve. Real money moved.

  1. 1Swapped about $1 of ETH into 0.9995 USDC.e to pay the fee. See it on Arbiscan
  2. 2Allowed Hegic's pool to take up to $0.42 for the fee. See it on Arbiscan
  3. 3Locked it. Paid a $0.40 fee and got card 16685: 0.00024543 BTC frozen at $81,486.80 until 24 October 2026. See it on Arbiscan

Open card 16685 to see what it is worth right now, read live from the chain.

The fork run

Simulated on a local fork of Arbitrum mainnet

Before going live we copied Arbitrum mainnet to a local test chain at block 515,564,054 and ran a full freeze there, so we could fake a price rise and watch the payout. No real money moved in this run.

  1. 1Asked the pool for a fee: 0.04 ETH for 60 days cost 8.09 USDC.
  2. 2Bought it. The test buyer paid exactly the quote and got a freeze card in their wallet.
  3. 3The card stored the frozen price: 0.04 ETH at $2,418.67.
  4. 4Faked a price of $2,900 on the fork. The card showed a payout of 19.25 USDC, which is ($2,900 minus $2,418.67) times 0.04.
  5. 5A stranger could not cash the card. The owner could, and received 19.25 USDC.

The fork card lived only on that test chain. Its number was later taken on mainnet by the real freeze above.

What is not proven yet

Card 16685 has not ended or been cashed on mainnet yet. Cashing a card that is worth something was proven on the fork, and on mainnet it waits for BTC to rise above $81,486.80 before 24 October.
