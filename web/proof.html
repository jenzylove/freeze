How a card pays out

Tested on a copy of Arbitrum mainnet

Both cards above settle when their owner cashes them before the end date. To test the whole payout before going live, we copied Arbitrum mainnet to a private test chain, bought a freeze and moved the price on that copy.

  1. 01Froze 0.04 ETH at $2,418.67 for 60 days. The fee was 8.09 USDC, exactly the quote.
  2. 02Set the ETH price on the copy to $2,900. The card showed 19.25 USDC, which is ($2,900 minus $2,418.67) times 0.04.
  3. 03A stranger tried to cash the card and was refused. The owner cashed it and received 19.25 USDC.
